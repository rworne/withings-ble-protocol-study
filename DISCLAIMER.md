# Research Disclaimer

## Purpose

The Withings BLE Protocol Study is an independent educational and interoperability research project.

Its purpose is to document the Bluetooth Low Energy communication protocols used by selected Withings devices, including discovery, authentication, provisioning, configuration, and measurement retrieval.

The findings are provided to assist researchers and developers in understanding and independently verifying observed protocol behavior.

## Experimental Methodology

The research was performed using commercially available Withings devices and standard Bluetooth development tools.

During the experiments:

- Devices were not jailbroken or physically modified.
- Device firmware was not modified.
- No encryption protections were defeated or bypassed.
- Independent provisioning software communicated with devices exclusively over Bluetooth Low Energy and did not interact with Withings servers.
- Protocol analysis included observations of normal communication by the official Withings application.
- Devices were restored to factory condition and re-provisioned according to Withings requirements at the conclusion of the experiments.

All recovered measurements used for documentation were obtained from devices and data under the researcher's control.

## Scope and Limitations

This project documents observed behavior of undocumented interfaces.

Findings may depend on device model, firmware version, regional configuration, and other factors.

No guarantee is made that documented commands or procedures will work on other devices or firmware revisions.

The project does not claim to provide a complete specification of the Withings protocol.

## Privacy and Security

Published examples are intended to exclude identifying information, authentication credentials, private keys, and other sensitive material.

Researchers should work only with devices and data they own or are authorized to access.

Raw Bluetooth captures and device databases should be reviewed and sanitized before publication.
